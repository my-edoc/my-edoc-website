About Us

Combining decades of engineering expertise with modern virtual assistance and AI-powered outsourcing solutions.

From International Construction Projects to Smart Digital Outsourcing

my-edoc.com was established to provide reliable and efficient outsourcing solutions for businesses, ecommerce stores, engineering consultants and construction companies.

With more than 35 years of experience in civil engineering, project management, QA/QC and technical documentation, we understand the importance of accuracy, organization, deadlines and professional communication.

Our experience spans international projects in the Philippines, Dubai and Qatar involving airports, infrastructure, commercial buildings and high-rise developments.

Today, we combine that technical expertise with modern digital tools, AI-assisted workflows and virtual assistant services to help businesses work smarter and faster.
